Business Memo

The Product, Marketing, and Customer Success teams.

The key churn drivers identified from the exploratory analysis:Key churn drivers identified from the exploratory analysis:

Objective

This analysis aimed to find out any behaviors of the customer leading to churn and important key factors which should be prioritized before initiating any retention campaign.

Key Findings

The number 1 indicator of churn is 1: Customer Inactivity.It is the question of Customer Inactivity that is the strongest churn indicator.

The average recency for customers who churned was 135 days, while retained customers had an average recency of 45 days.

Implication:
Before a customer "churns," they are already disengaged.

High Risk is #2 One-Time Buyers.One-Time Buyers are High Risk #2.

The average number of purchases churned customers made was 1.10, whereas the retained customers averaged 2.03 purchases.

Implication:
There seems to be a important stage between the first purchase and repeat purchase for long-term retention.

Low-spending customers churn even more often as a group.

The average revenue of retained customers was at ₹1,554.73 whereas the average revenue of churned customers was at ₹756.22.

Implication:
As the value of the customer increases, so does brand loyalty.

#4: Lower Website/App Engagement Indicates future churn.

The average number of sessions retained customers had in the past 30 days was 6.73, whereas the average number of sessions churned customers had in the past 30 days was 4.02.

Implication:
A lack of digital engagement can be a precursor to churn.

There is a need to continue monitoring support and return behaviours.Support and return behaviours to be further monitored.

Future customer satisfaction monitoring should include support interactions and return activity to gain further understanding of customer dissatisfaction.

Recommended Retention Priorities

Inactive Customer Re-Engagement is the top priority.

Target customers who have high recency scores and decreasing activity.

Suggested actions:

- Win-back email campaigns
- Personalized recommendations
- Loyalty reminders

Priority 2: Increase Second-Purchase Conversion

Consider only customers who have made a single purchase in the last few days.

Suggested actions:

- Post-purchase journeys
- Product replenishment reminders
- Bundle offers

Priority 3: Monitor Digital Engagement

Monitor customer sessions, views of products and interactions with campaigns.

Suggested actions:

- Engagement-based triggers
- Personalized content
- Product discovery campaigns

Points for further investigation:

- Churn by acquisition channel
Customer churn by loyalty level
The effects of sentiment on support ticket handling on retention.How sentiment affects support ticket handling and retention.
As a rule, the greater the percentage of returns, the lesser the churn.In general, the higher the percentage of return, the lower the churn.
- Customer segment effectiveness of the campaign

Conclusion

According to the analysis, inactivity, a low frequency of purchases and declining engagement are all closely correlated with churn, as is lower customer value. Retention strategies should focus on reengaging customers before they become dormant and on repeating their purchases.Customer retention should put the focus on getting customers to purchase again before they become dormant and on customer identification at the early stages who are not engaged in the purchase.
